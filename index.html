Intro

I was born and raised in Mahajanga, on the north-west coast of Madagascar. In April 2025 I moved to Mauritius to study for a BSc (Hons) in Software Engineering at the University of Technology, Mauritius. Starting over in a new country taught me to adapt quickly and to take on challenges that first look out of reach.

My studies cover the full path of a software project: object-oriented programming and algorithms in Java, relational databases with Oracle, web applications with ASP.NET and mobile development with Kotlin. Alongside my courses I took part in the development of an Odoo system for a music school, and in a 24-hour web development hackathon at Middlesex University Mauritius.

Outside the classroom I play piano, drums and guitar with the KLP Band, and I enjoy football and basketball.
